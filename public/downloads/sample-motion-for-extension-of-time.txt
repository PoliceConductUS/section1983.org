PLAINTIFF'S UNOPPOSED MOTION FOR EXTENSION OF TIME
TO RESPOND TO DEFENDANTS' MOTION TO DISMISS

Plaintiff [Your Name], proceeding without a lawyer, asks the Court to extend the
deadline to respond to Defendants' Motion to Dismiss (Dkt. [__]) from [current
date] to [requested date]. Plaintiff states:

1. Defendants filed their Motion to Dismiss on [date]. Dkt. [__]. Under Local
   Rule [__], Plaintiff's response is due [current date].

2. This is Plaintiff's first request to extend this deadline.

3. Defendants' motion is [__] pages and attaches a [__]-page appendix. It raises
   qualified immunity for [number] individual defendants and cites [number]
   cases. Plaintiff needs additional time to review the appendix, research the
   cited authority, and prepare a response that addresses each ground.

4. Plaintiff asks for [number] additional days, to [requested date]. Plaintiff
   believes this is the full amount of time needed and does not anticipate a
   second request.

5. On [date], Plaintiff contacted Defendants' counsel, [name], by [email or
   phone]. Counsel stated that Defendants do not oppose this request.

6. No scheduling order has been entered. The extension will not affect any
   other deadline or the trial setting.

For these reasons, Plaintiff asks the Court to extend the deadline to respond to
Defendants' Motion to Dismiss to [requested date]. A proposed order is attached.
