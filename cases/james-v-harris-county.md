## What Happened

Deputy William Wilkinson shot and killed Hiji Harrison during a traffic stop. Harrison's family sued Harris County, arguing that inadequate investigations of officer-involved shootings encouraged excessive force. After the jury deadlocked on the force question, the district court entered judgment for the county. The Fifth Circuit affirmed.

Wilkinson said Harrison consented to a search, a pistol was found, and Harrison struggled during handcuffing in the patrol car. The family disputed that account with physical evidence. The county's liability presented a separate question: whether its alleged investigation practice caused this shooting. [Read the opinion, pages 2–4](https://www.ca5.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/07/07-20725-CV0.wpd.pdf).

## What the Court Decided

The court assumed for its analysis that excessive force occurred and that the challenged investigation practice existed. It resolved the case on [causation](/terms-and-concepts/moving-force-causation): the evidence failed to connect that practice to Wilkinson's decision to shoot.

For the family's theory that lax investigations encouraged force by creating an expectation that excessive force would go unpunished, the missing link was evidence that Wilkinson knew about the practice. Such knowledge might be shown through evidence of widespread departmental awareness, but the record was insufficient. See opinion pages 8–10.

## What the Judge and Defense Will Examine

For this kind of [municipal-liability](/terms-and-concepts/municipal-liability) theory, explain how the alleged investigation practice affected the conduct that injured you. Identify the chain of events and the evidence supporting each link.

The county's defense focused on the missing connection between its practices and this shooting. Evidence of deficient investigations must support the particular causal theory you advance. The officer-knowledge discussion in [James](/cases/james-v-harris-county) addresses the family's theory that lax investigations encouraged excessive force. Other [Monell](/cases/monell-v-dep-t-of-soc-servs) theories require their own causal showing.

## How to Use the Decision

Separate evidence of the underlying use of force, the municipal practice, and the connection between them. A factual dispute about force can remain unresolved while the city or county wins on a missing element of its separate liability.
