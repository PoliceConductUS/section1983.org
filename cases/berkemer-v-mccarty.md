## What Happened

A trooper stopped McCarty's car, questioned him at the roadside, arrested him, and questioned him further at the jail. The case required the Court to distinguish the traffic stop from the later custodial questioning.

## What the Court Decided

[Miranda's](/cases/miranda-v-arizona) safeguards apply to custodial interrogation for misdemeanors as well as felonies. An ordinary traffic stop generally involves a temporary, public restraint that falls short of Miranda custody. If restraint becomes comparable to formal arrest, the custody analysis changes. [Read the opinion, 468 U.S. 428–442](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-468/pdf/USREPORTS-468-420.pdf).

## How to Use the Decision

Separate the stages of your encounter. Record where questioning occurred, what restraints were used, how long it lasted, and what officers said. The judge will examine the circumstances at the time of each statement. The defense may argue that roadside questions occurred before custody began.

Read the [Miranda guide](/terms-and-concepts/miranda) to distinguish warnings, suppression, and civil damages.
