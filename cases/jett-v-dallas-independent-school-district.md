## What This Case Is About

Norman Jett, a white teacher and football coach, claimed that his reassignment by Dallas Independent School District resulted from racial discrimination. His case concerned damages for rights protected by § 1981, a statute protecting equal rights in activities including making and enforcing contracts, and the route for enforcing those rights against state actors.

The Supreme Court held that [§ 1983](/terms-and-concepts/section-1983) supplies the exclusive federal damages remedy for the § 1981 violation alleged against the state actors. The district's responsibility therefore had to satisfy [Monell](/cases/monell-v-dep-t-of-soc-servs).

## The Facts

Jett alleged that his principal's recommendation to remove him from coaching duties was racially motivated. The superintendent approved the recommendation and reassigned him. A jury found for Jett, but the appeal raised questions about the instructions governing the school district's liability.

## What the Court Decided

The district could be held responsible under § 1983 for its own policy or custom, rather than automatically for an employee's discriminatory decision. The Court rejected using § 1981 to obtain broader employer liability on the claims before it. [Read the opinion, 491 U.S. 735–736](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-491/pdf/USREPORTS-491-701.pdf).

The Court also remanded for analysis of who held final policymaking authority. The judge identifies that authority from state and local law. An official's discretion to make a particular employment decision and authority to establish governmental policy are different questions. See pages 737–738.

## What the Judge and Defense Will Examine

For a § 1981 damages claim against a state actor, address the § 1983 framework. For a city or school-district defendant, identify the policy, custom, or [final policymaker](/terms-and-concepts/final-policymaker) decision that caused the violation.

The defense will distinguish an employee's decision from governmental policy. Identify the relevant legal allocation of authority and any review or delegation that matters to the decision. A job title alone leaves that analysis incomplete.

## How to Use the Decision

Use [Jett](/cases/jett-v-dallas-independent-school-district) for its § 1981 damages holding and its policymaker analysis. Other federal statutes have their own enforcement rules.
