## What the Rule Does

Miranda warnings protect the [Fifth Amendment](/terms-and-concepts/fifth-amendment) privilege against self-incrimination during custodial interrogation. Before that questioning, officers generally must explain your right to remain silent, the possible use of your statements, and your right to a lawyer, including an appointed lawyer if you cannot afford one. [Miranda v. Arizona](/cases/miranda-v-arizona) established these safeguards.

For your case, distinguish a request to **suppress** a statement—keep it out of the prosecution's evidence—from a civil claim seeking damages. The requirements and remedies differ.

## What You Should Be Able to Identify

Build a timeline showing custody, each question, warnings, any agreement to answer, and the use of the statement. That lets you test three issues: whether warnings were required, whether an exception applies, and what remedy the alleged violation supports.

## Custody and Interrogation Are Separate Questions

**Custody** means formal arrest or restraint comparable to arrest, assessed from the circumstances. An ordinary traffic stop can restrain your movement without amounting to Miranda custody. Record the location, duration, restraints, officers' statements, and other facts affecting the encounter. [Berkemer v. McCarty](/cases/berkemer-v-mccarty) explains that traffic-stop distinction.

**Interrogation** includes direct questioning and police words or conduct they should know are reasonably likely to draw an incriminating response. Volunteered statements require a different analysis. [Pennsylvania v. Muniz](/cases/pennsylvania-v-muniz) examines questioning during processing after arrest.

## What the Defense Will Say

The defense may argue that you were outside custody, volunteered the statement, or knowingly and voluntarily waived your rights after warnings. Compare each position with the recording and the sequence of events.

It may also rely on an exception. [New York v. Quarles](/cases/new-york-v-quarles) permits limited unwarned questions reasonably prompted by a public-safety danger. Identify the danger that existed at that moment and how the question addressed it. Routine booking questions require their own analysis; [Muniz](/cases/pennsylvania-v-muniz) explains why a question's content and purpose matter.

## How This Affects a § 1983 Claim

[Vega v. Tekoh](/cases/vega-v-tekoh) forecloses a § 1983 claim based solely on a violation of Miranda's warning rules, even when the statement was admitted in the criminal case. If your record supports a different constitutional claim involving coercion or other misconduct, explain the facts and requirements of that claim separately. [Read Vega's holding](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/21pdf/21-499_gfbh.pdf).
